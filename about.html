How the T20 World Cup Began

The ICC Men’s T20 World Cup was introduced to bring a fast-paced and entertainment-driven format of cricket to a global audience.

The inaugural tournament in 2007 marked a major turning point in international cricket, capturing the attention of millions of fans worldwide and redefining how the game is played and enjoyed.

Today, the T20 World Cup stands as one of the most exciting global sporting events, showcasing top talent, thrilling finishes and unforgettable moments.

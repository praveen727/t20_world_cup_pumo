MA Chidambaram Stadium

Chennai, India

Ground Information
  • 📍 City: Chennai
  • 🏟 Capacity: 38,000
  • 📅 Established: 1916
  • 🏏 Pitch Type: Spin Friendly
  • 🌙 Best For: Day Matches
About the Ground

MA Chidambaram Stadium, popularly known as Chepauk, is one of the oldest and most historic cricket stadiums in India. It is renowned for its slow pitch that assists spinners, especially as matches progress.

Chepauk has hosted several iconic Test and limited-overs matches and is known for its knowledgeable and passionate cricket crowd.

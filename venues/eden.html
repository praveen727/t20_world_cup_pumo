Eden Gardens

Kolkata, India

Ground Information
  • 📍 City: Kolkata
  • 🏟 Capacity: 68,000
  • 📅 Established: 1864
  • 🏏 Pitch Type: Balanced (Bat & Ball)
  • 🌙 Best For: Night Matches
About the Ground

Eden Gardens is often referred to as the “Mecca of Indian Cricket”. It is one of the largest and most iconic cricket stadiums in the world, known for its electrifying atmosphere and passionate crowd.

The stadium has hosted historic Test matches, World Cup games, and memorable limited-overs encounters, making it a jewel in Indian cricket history.

M. Chinnaswamy Stadium

Bengaluru, India

Ground Information
  • 📍 City: Bengaluru
  • 🏟 Capacity: 40,000
  • 📅 Established: 1969
  • 🏏 Pitch Type: Batting Friendly
  • 🌙 Best For: High-Scoring Night Matches
About the Ground

M. Chinnaswamy Stadium is famous for its short boundaries and batting-friendly surface, making it one of the most exciting venues for T20 cricket in the world.

The stadium regularly hosts high-scoring matches and is known for its lively crowd and electric atmosphere during night games.
